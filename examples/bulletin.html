Hollowstone Restored

A fan patch for Dustline. It brings back the cut quests, the lost dialog and the flooded Cistern under the city of the burned.

What the patch changes

The burned of Hollowstone had more to say than the shipped game lets them. The game files still hold their lines, three unused maps and six quests that the team cut before release.

This patch connects that content again. You can enter the Cistern from the sewers, help Odell's family get out, and decide who controls the water pump after you take the chip.

The patch does not change the main story or the ending slides. If a line or a map was not in the game files, it is not in the patch.

Install

  1. Make a copy of your Dustline folder.
  2. Download hollowstone-restored-1.4.zip (48 MB).
  3. Unzip it into the Dustline folder and replace the files.
  4. Start a new game. Old saves do not get the new maps.
